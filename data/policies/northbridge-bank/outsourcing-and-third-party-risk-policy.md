# Outsourcing and Third-Party Risk Management Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope
1.1 This Policy sets the minimum standards for identifying, approving, contracting, monitoring and
exiting outsourcing and other third-party arrangements at Northbridge Bank plc ("the Bank"). It
applies to all business units, to arrangements with intra-group and external service providers,
and to cloud services.

1.2 An arrangement is classified as material where a failure of the service would materially
impair the Bank's compliance with its regulatory obligations, its financial performance, or the
continuity of its services to customers. Material arrangements are subject to all sections of
this Policy.

## 2. Accountability
2.1 The Board retains ultimate responsibility for all outsourced activities. Outsourcing an
activity never transfers the Bank's regulatory responsibilities, and senior managers may not
delegate their accountability for an outsourced function to a service provider.

2.2 The Chief Operating Officer (SMF24) is accountable for this Policy and for the Bank's
outsourcing framework. Each material arrangement has a named business owner.

2.3 Before entering into a material arrangement, the business owner shall document how the
arrangement avoids undue additional operational risk and confirm that it will not materially
impair the quality of the Bank's internal control or the ability of the Bank's regulators to
monitor its compliance.

## 3. Due diligence and approval
3.1 Before selecting a service provider for a material arrangement, the Bank shall carry out
proportionate due diligence on the provider's financial soundness, ability, capacity, resources,
organisational structure, information security, business continuity arrangements and any
authorisations or licences required to perform the service.

3.2 Material arrangements require approval by the Executive Risk Committee and notification to
the Board Risk Committee.

## 4. Contractual requirements
4.1 Every material arrangement shall be governed by a written agreement that clearly sets out the
respective rights and obligations of the Bank and the service provider, service levels, data
protection and confidentiality terms.

4.2 The agreement shall give the Bank, its internal and external auditors and its regulators
rights of audit and of access to the provider's books, records and premises relevant to the
service.

4.3 The agreement shall require the provider to inform the Bank of any material sub-outsourcing
of the service.

## 5. Ongoing oversight
5.1 Each material arrangement shall be reviewed annually by its business owner against the agreed
service levels, with results reported to the Executive Risk Committee.

5.2 Where a service provider fails to meet service levels or regulatory requirements, the business
owner shall escalate the issue to the Executive Risk Committee and agree a remediation plan,
which may include termination of the arrangement.

## 6. Exit planning
6.1 For every material arrangement the Bank shall maintain a documented exit plan, tested where
practicable, that allows the service to be transferred to another provider or brought in-house
without undue disruption.

## 7. Review of this Policy
7.1 This Policy is reviewed by the Board Risk Committee at least every two years.

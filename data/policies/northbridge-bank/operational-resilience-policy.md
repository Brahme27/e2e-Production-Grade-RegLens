# Operational Resilience Policy

## 1. Purpose
1.1 This Policy describes how Northbridge Bank plc ("the Bank") prevents, adapts to, responds to,
recovers from and learns from operational disruptions, so that it can continue to deliver its
most important services to customers and the market.

## 2. Important business services
2.1 The Bank shall identify its important business services: the services it provides to external
customers or market participants whose disruption could cause intolerable harm to consumers or
risk to market integrity, the Bank's safety and soundness or financial stability.

2.2 The list of important business services shall be reviewed every two years, or earlier where
there is a material change to the Bank's business.

## 3. Impact tolerances
3.1 For each important business service the Bank shall set an impact tolerance: the maximum
tolerable level of disruption, expressed at least as a maximum duration of outage.

3.2 The Bank shall ensure it is able to remain within its impact tolerance for each important
business service in the event of a severe but plausible disruption to its operations, and shall
invest to close any vulnerabilities that prevent this.

## 4. Mapping
4.1 For each important business service the Bank shall identify and document the people,
processes, technology, facilities and information required to deliver it, in sufficient detail
to identify vulnerabilities and dependencies on third parties.

## 5. Scenario testing
5.1 Each year the Bank shall carry out scenario tests for a selection of its important business
services, chosen by the Operational Resilience Working Group, to assess whether it can remain
within impact tolerance in severe but plausible scenarios such as a cyber attack, loss of a key
supplier or loss of a site.

## 6. Self-assessment
6.1 The Bank shall maintain a written self-assessment documenting its important business services,
impact tolerances and the justification for them, its mapping and its testing results.

6.2 Each version of the self-assessment shall be retained for three years.

6.3 The Board shall approve the self-assessment at least annually.

## 7. Governance
7.1 The Chief Operating Officer (SMF24) is accountable for operational resilience. The Operational
Resilience Working Group meets monthly and reports to the Executive Risk Committee each quarter.

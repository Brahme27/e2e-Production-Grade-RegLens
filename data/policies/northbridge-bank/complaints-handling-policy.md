# Complaints Handling Policy

## 1. Principles
1.1 Complaints are handled fairly, consistently and promptly, and root causes are analysed to
improve products and outcomes for retail customers, in line with the Consumer Duty.

## 2. Process
2.1 Complaints may be made by any reasonable means. Each complaint is acknowledged promptly and
investigated competently, diligently and impartially.

2.2 A final response is sent within eight weeks of receipt, or a holding response explaining the
delay and the customer's right to refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

2.3 Complaints resolved by the close of the third business day after receipt may be closed with a
summary resolution communication.

## 3. Reporting
3.1 Complaints data is reported to the FCA twice a year in the prescribed format, and to the Board
quarterly together with root-cause analysis.

# Know Your Customer and Anti-Money Laundering Policy

## 1. Customer acceptance
1.1 No account shall be opened in an anonymous or fictitious name, or where the Bank is unable to
apply appropriate customer due diligence measures.

1.2 Customers are classified as low, medium or high risk based on identity, occupation, location,
nature of business and transaction profile.

## 2. Customer due diligence
2.1 Identity and address shall be verified using an Officially Valid Document, Aadhaar-based
e-KYC with consent, or video-based customer identification.

2.2 Beneficial owners of legal entities shall be identified using the thresholds prescribed by the
Prevention of Money-laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules.

## 3. Periodic updation
3.1 KYC shall be updated at least once every two years for high-risk customers, every eight years
for medium-risk customers and every ten years for low-risk customers.

## 4. Monitoring and reporting
4.1 Transactions shall be monitored against the customer's profile. Suspicious transactions shall
be reported to FIU-IND by the Principal Officer within seven working days of being so concluded.

4.2 Cash transactions above the prescribed threshold shall be reported to FIU-IND monthly.

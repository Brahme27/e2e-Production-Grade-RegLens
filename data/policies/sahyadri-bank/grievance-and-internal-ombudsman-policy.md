# Customer Grievance Redressal and Internal Ombudsman Policy

## 1. Purpose and scope
1.1 This Policy sets out how Sahyadri Bank Ltd ("the Bank") receives, records, resolves and
reviews customer complaints, and how the office of the Internal Ombudsman (IO) operates, in line
with the Reserve Bank of India's directions on the Internal Ombudsman framework.

1.2 The Policy applies to all branches, digital channels, call centres and business units of
the Bank and to all staff and outsourced agents who handle customer complaints.

## 2. Appointment and eligibility of the Internal Ombudsman
2.1 The Bank shall appoint at least one Internal Ombudsman. The Internal Ombudsman shall be a
retired or serving officer of the rank of General Manager or equivalent in a regulated entity,
the Reserve Bank or another financial sector regulator, with at least seven years of experience
in banking, regulation, supervision, payment systems, credit information or consumer protection.
A serving officer must relinquish that position before taking charge.

2.2 The Internal Ombudsman shall not have been employed by the Bank or any of its holding,
associate or subsidiary companies, and shall not be so employed during the term.

2.3 A person shall not be appointed as Internal Ombudsman if he or she is above 65 years of age
on the date of appointment.

2.4 The Bank may appoint a Deputy Internal Ombudsman (Dy. IO) who shall be a retired or serving
officer of the rank of Deputy General Manager or equivalent with at least five years of relevant
experience. The Dy. IO reports functionally to the Internal Ombudsman.

## 3. Tenure, vacancy and remuneration
3.1 The Internal Ombudsman and Dy. IO are appointed on a contractual basis for a fixed term of
three years, which may be extended or renewed once, provided the total tenure does not exceed
five years.

3.2 During the temporary absence of the Internal Ombudsman, the Dy. IO shall discharge the
functions of the Internal Ombudsman.

3.3 Where the office of the Internal Ombudsman falls vacant before the end of the term, the Bank
shall appoint a successor meeting the eligibility criteria in section 2 within three months.

3.4 The Customer Service Committee of the Board shall approve the remuneration and facilities of
the Internal Ombudsman and Dy. IO; these shall not be reduced during the term.

## 4. Reporting lines and oversight
4.1 The Internal Ombudsman reports administratively to the Executive Director in charge of
customer service (the Competent Authority) and functionally to the Customer Service Committee of
the Board.

4.2 The Internal Ombudsman shall present a report on the activities of the office, including an
analysis of complaints, to the Customer Service Committee of the Board every quarter.

4.3 The office of the Internal Ombudsman shall analyse the pattern of complaints received against
the Bank by product, category, customer group and geography every six months and share its
findings with the business heads for corrective action.

4.4 The Internal Audit Department shall audit the implementation of this Policy and of the Internal
Ombudsman framework once a year, including the adequacy of staff and infrastructure of the IO
office and the timeliness of escalations. The audit shall not assess the correctness of the
Internal Ombudsman's decisions.

4.5 Information on complaints decided by the RBI Ombudsman in favour of complainants shall be
placed before the Customer Service Committee every quarter, with an analysis of the top five
complaint categories and remedial actions.

## 5. Handling and escalation of complaints
5.1 All complaints are recorded in the Bank's Complaint Management System (CMS). Each complaint
receives a unique reference number, acknowledged to the customer within one working day.

5.2 Complaints that the Bank proposes to reject wholly or resolve only partially shall be reviewed
by an officer not below the rank of Assistant General Manager, who did not handle the original
complaint, before being escalated.

5.3 The CMS shall automatically escalate every wholly rejected or partially resolved complaint to
the office of the Internal Ombudsman within 25 days of receipt of the complaint.

5.4 The Bank shall communicate its final decision to the complainant within 30 days of receiving
the complaint.

5.5 Where, after review by the Internal Ombudsman, a complaint remains wholly rejected or partially
resolved, the Bank's reply shall state that the complaint has been reviewed by the Internal
Ombudsman and shall inform the complainant of the option to approach the RBI Ombudsman, including
the address of the Reserve Bank's Complaint Management System portal (https://cms.rbi.org.in)
and of the Centralised Receipt and Processing Centre.

5.6 To make the grievance mechanism easy to reach, the name, email address and telephone number of
the Internal Ombudsman shall be displayed on the Bank's website and in all branches.

## 6. Reporting to the Reserve Bank
6.1 The Bank shall inform the Consumer Education and Protection Department of the Reserve Bank of
the appointment of an Internal Ombudsman or Dy. IO, with the details prescribed by the Reserve
Bank, within 15 days of the appointment.

6.2 The Bank shall submit the prescribed quarterly report on the functioning of the Internal
Ombudsman to the Consumer Education and Protection Department on or before the 15th day of the
month following each quarter.

## 7. Review of this Policy
7.1 This Policy shall be reviewed by the Customer Service Committee of the Board every two years,
or earlier if required by changes in regulation.
